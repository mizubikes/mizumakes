How this works

  • Every morning a script checks each brand's own online store for a 1kg, 1.75mm spool and records the price per kg.
  • 90-day range is the lowest and highest store price seen in the last 90 days. Deal means the store has it marked down, or it's at least 10% under its typical price. 90-day low needs two weeks of history before it shows.
  • Amazon prices aren't listed because they change through the day — tap the Amazon button to see yours. Amazon links are affiliate links; buying through them supports the site at no extra cost to you.
  • Shipping, tax, and color differences aren't included. Some colors cost more.

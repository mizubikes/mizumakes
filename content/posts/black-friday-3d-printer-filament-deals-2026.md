## The picks worth buying

![Bambu Lab P2S](/images/bambu-lab-p2s.webp)
*Bambu Lab P2S. Image: Bambu Lab*

**Best for most people: [Bambu Lab P2S Combo](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Bambu+Lab+P2S+Combo&tag=mizubikes-web-20), $649.** That's $100 off, and it includes the AMS 2 Pro, which dries filament. It's enclosed, fast, and runs PLA, PETG, ABS, and ASA. If you're choosing between it and the cheaper P1S AMS 2 Pro Combo at $509, my [P1S vs P2S breakdown](/posts/bambu-p1s-sale-vs-p2s/) covers the trade-off.

![Flashforge Creator 5 Pro Black Friday sale](/images/flashforge-creator-5-pro-black-friday.webp)
*Flashforge applies the discount automatically in the cart. Image: Flashforge*

**Best multi-material for functional parts: [Flashforge Creator 5 Pro](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Flashforge+Creator+5+Pro&tag=mizubikes-web-20), $749.** Four independent toolheads, a 65 °C heated chamber, and hardened nozzles. $749 is below its $799 launch price and the lowest it's been on Flashforge's own store. It's the printer [I chose over the Bambu H2D](/posts/why-i-chose-flashforge-creator-5-pro/), and I wrote a [multi-color setup guide](/posts/creator-5-pro-multicolor-printing-guide/) for it.

**Best budget enclosed printer: Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2, $319.** It's an enclosed CoreXY printer at a price where most printers are open-frame, which matters if you want to try ASA or ABS. See my [best budget 3D printer under $300](/posts/best-budget-3d-printer-under-300/) guide for the trade-offs at this price.

**Cheapest toolchanger: Snapmaker U1, from $699.** Four toolheads for $50 less than the Creator 5 Pro, but it isn't enclosed and has no heated chamber, so it's a PLA, PETG, and TPU machine. My [Creator 5 Pro comparison](/posts/flashforge-creator-5-pro-deal-vs-h2d-indx-u1-p2s/) puts the two side by side.

**Big build volume and high-temp materials: [Bambu Lab H2D Combo](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Bambu+Lab+H2D+AMS+Combo&tag=mizubikes-web-20), $1,599.** That's the lowest price it has sold for. It gives you 350 × 320 × 325 mm, a 350 °C nozzle, and a 65 °C chamber. The [Bambu lineup post](/posts/bambu-lab-new-printers-2026/) explains how it compares with the H2S and X2D.

![Prusa CORE One+ Black Friday offer: gifts up to $450 and free shipping](/images/prusa-core-one-plus-black-friday-2026.webp)
*Prusa's Black Friday offer for the CORE One+. Image: Prusa Research*

**Best if you want a Prusa: CORE One+ (Gen 2), $999 kit or $1,299 assembled, plus gifts.** Prusa isn't discounting the printer itself. Instead, it adds extras it values at up to $450: an OpenPrintTag upgrade, a 1 kg spool of Prusament PLA, a one-year extended warranty, a $50 voucher toward the INDX toolchanger upgrade, four 3D models, two Prusa Academy courses, and a voucher for nozzle-cleaning wires. Shipping is free on orders over $200. Most of that value is the warranty and the vouchers, so treat it as a sweetener rather than a $450 price cut. If you're choosing between the two most common enclosed picks, my [Prusa CORE One vs Bambu Lab P2S comparison](/posts/prusa-core-one-vs-bambu-lab-p2s/) covers the trade-offs, and the [CORE One+ heated chamber post](/posts/prusa-core-one-plus-heated-chamber-features/) explains what the "+" adds.

**Skip if you're waiting for the H3C to drop:** Bambu's new [H3C nine-tool changer](/posts/bambu-lab-h3c-toolchanger-specs-price/) launched November 3 at $2,499 and isn't part of the sale.

For everyday PLA and PETG, the Elegoo 10 kg bundles are the deal to beat. At about $9/kg they're cheaper than any single spool on my [filament price tracker](/filament-prices/). Elegoo also gives new customers an extra 5% on 10 kg bundles.

For functional parts, the Polymaker multi-buy is the better fit. It covers its PETG, ASA, and Fiberon carbon-fiber lines, which you won't find in a budget bundle. Polymaker also adds a free mystery spool above a minimum spend. If you're stocking up on engineering materials, Prusament's 40% off PC Blend is a big discount on a material that rarely goes on sale. Remember to dry it before printing; my [filament drying guide](/posts/how-to-dry-filament-dryer-box-vs-dehydrator-vs-heated-bed/) covers temperatures.

Carbon-fiber filaments wear out brass nozzles, so if you're buying PA-CF or PETG-CF on sale, read [when you need a hardened steel nozzle](/posts/hardened-steel-nozzle-abrasive-filaments/) first.

Accessories worth grabbing: the Bambu AMS 2 Pro is $229 on its own, and Flashforge has 20% off accessories, including Creator 5 nozzle assemblies at $31.92.

## Should you buy now or wait?

Buy now if your printer is on the list above. Most brands set their Black Friday prices in late October and are holding them through the end of November. The exceptions are flash rounds, which can drop a single model lower for a day or two. Bambu's land on Tuesdays, and Elegoo runs two-day rounds about once a week (dates in the table above). If you're eyeing a specific model, check during those windows.

Two things to watch. First, Bambu's 30-day price protection excludes flash sales and limited-time promotions, so don't count on a refund if the price dips after you buy. Second, the Flashforge and Prusa sales end November 30, a day before Bambu's. If you're cross-shopping the Creator 5 Pro and a Bambu, don't leave it to the last day.

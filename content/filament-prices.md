Filament prices move a lot, and "sale" banners don't always mean much. This page checks each brand's own store every morning and logs the price per kilogram, so you can see what a spool normally costs and whether today's price is actually low.

**How to read it:** the price shown is the brand's own store price for a 1kg, 1.75mm spool. Amazon prices often differ and change through the day, so use the Amazon button to check yours. A **Deal** tag means the price is on sale or at least 10% under its 90-day typical price.
